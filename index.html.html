About

I'm Faris — a polytechnic student passionate about robotics, embedded systems, and hands-on engineering. I developed MAX-E2, a multi-sport humanoid robot with computer vision for weightlifting, basketball, and archery events, and have competed at both national and international level in FIRA robotics competitions.

Beyond the lab, I've served as a facilitator for the TechnoHuffaz Program — conducting Arduino and electronics workshops — and was selected as one of four student representatives for a national media interview by KPT (Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi). I'm always looking for new challenges and opportunities to grow in the field of electronics and robotics engineering.

Robotics

MAX-E2 Multi-Sport Humanoid Robot

Designed and developed a multi-sport humanoid robot capable of performing three sport-inspired tasks: weightlifting, basketball, and archery. Built on a ROBOTIS platform with DYNAMIXEL actuators and CM-550 controller, integrated with a Raspberry Pi 4 running Python and OpenCV for real-time object and target detection. Developed servo-controlled task sequences for each event with a custom vision processing pipeline.
